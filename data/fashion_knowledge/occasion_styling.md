# Occasion-Based Styling

This document describes how formality, clothing, footwear, accessories and color direction can change across occasions. All recommendations are adaptable to climate, location, venue and personal preference. When an invitation or venue states a dress code, that always takes priority.

## Reading Formality

- Formality is a spectrum from relaxed (casual) to highly structured (business formal, ceremonial).
- Venue, time of day, hosts' expectations and local customs all shift where an outfit lands on that spectrum.
- When unsure, dressing one step more polished than expected is often easier than the reverse.

## Casual

### Formality
- Low. Comfort and personal expression lead.

### Clothing
- T-shirts, polos, casual shirts, jeans, chinos, shorts, casual dresses, kurtis, joggers.

### Footwear
- Sneakers, sandals, flats, casual slip-ons.

### Accessories
- Simple watch, cap, sunglasses, casual bag.

### Color Direction
- Open. Neutrals with one or two color accents are an easy base.

### Common Mistakes
- Wearing worn-out or ill-fitting basics that look unintentional.
- Mixing too many patterns without a unifying color.

## Smart Casual

### Formality
- Low to medium. Relaxed but clearly put together.

### Clothing
- Fitted or regular shirts, knit polos, tailored chinos, dark jeans, unstructured blazers, midi dresses, kurtas with tailored trousers.

### Footwear
- Clean leather sneakers, loafers, derbies, ankle boots, neat flats.

### Accessories
- Leather belt, minimal watch, simple jewelry.

### Color Direction
- Muted or earthy tones with medium contrast.

### Common Mistakes
- Mixing very casual items (graphic tees, worn sneakers) with tailored pieces.
- Over-formalizing with a full suit look.

## Office / Business Casual

### Formality
- Medium. Professional but not ceremonial.

### Clothing
- Shirts, blouses, chinos, tailored trousers, knee-length skirts, straight or A-line dresses, light blazers, formal kurtas or kurtis where the workplace allows.

### Footwear
- Loafers, derbies, closed flats, low block heels, formal-leaning sneakers where accepted.

### Accessories
- Belt, understated watch, small bag or laptop bag, minimal jewelry.

### Color Direction
- Navy, grey, beige, white, light blue, olive, muted pastels.

### Common Mistakes
- Ignoring the workplace's actual dress culture.
- Overly sheer, very tight or very worn garments.
- Heavy or noisy accessories.

## Formal / Business Formal

### Formality
- High. Structured, conservative and precise.

### Clothing
- Suits, dress shirts, formal trousers, pencil or straight skirts with a jacket, tailored dresses with a blazer, formal sarees in subdued fabrics.

### Footwear
- Oxfords, derbies, closed formal shoes, closed-toe pumps.

### Accessories
- Tie, belt matched to shoes, classic watch, minimal jewelry.

### Color Direction
- Navy, charcoal, black, grey, white and light blue.

### Common Mistakes
- Ill-fitting suit, especially in the shoulders and sleeve length.
- Mismatched belt and shoes.
- Casual footwear with formal clothing.

## Wedding

### Formality
- Medium to high, depending on the ceremony, community, time of day and venue.

### Clothing
- Men: sherwanis, bandhgalas, kurta sets with jackets, suits.
- Women: sarees, lehengas, anarkalis, salwar suits, gowns, festive Indo-western outfits.
- Non-binary or gender-neutral dressers can draw from any of the above.

### Footwear
- Mojaris, juttis, formal shoes, dressy sandals, heels or embellished flats. Comfort matters because events run long.

### Accessories
- Stoles, safas, brooches, jewelry, clutches, watches.

### Color Direction
- Rich jewel tones, pastels or metallics. Wedding hosts or community traditions sometimes shape color etiquette, so check when possible.

### Common Mistakes
- Wearing white or very plain looks where the community treats it as inappropriate, or wearing a color the couple has asked guests to avoid.
- Choosing outfits that are difficult to move, sit or eat in.
- Overshadowing the couple with the most elaborate outfit in the room.

## Reception

### Formality
- Medium to high, often more Western-leaning than the ceremony.

### Clothing
- Suits, tuxedo-style jackets, gowns, cocktail dresses, sarees with modern blouses, Indo-western outfits.

### Footwear
- Polished formal shoes, dressy heels, embellished loafers.

### Accessories
- Pocket squares, statement earrings or necklaces (one focal piece), clutches, dress watches.

### Color Direction
- Deeper evening tones such as wine, midnight blue, emerald, black or metallic accents.

### Common Mistakes
- Mixing two heavy statement pieces, such as a bold necklace with heavy earrings and a heavily embellished outfit.
- Wearing daytime-casual clothing to an evening event.

## Festival

### Formality
- Low to medium. Festive rather than strictly formal.

### Clothing
- Kurtas, kurta sets, sarees, salwar suits, ethnic dresses, Indo-western sets.

### Footwear
- Kolhapuris, juttis, mojaris, festive sandals, clean flats.

### Accessories
- Bangles, earrings, stoles, watches with simple straps.

### Color Direction
- Bright or rich colors and festive metallics. See regional notes below.

### Common Mistakes
- Ignoring weather. Heavy fabrics in warm, humid conditions are uncomfortable.
- Too many competing statement elements.

## Date / Social Evening

### Formality
- Low to medium. The goal is comfortable and polished.

### Clothing
- Well-fitted shirts, knitwear, dark jeans or tailored trousers, midi dresses, jumpsuits, co-ords, fusion kurtas.

### Footwear
- Loafers, clean sneakers, boots, block heels, neat flats.

### Accessories
- Simple watch, subtle jewelry, small bag.

### Color Direction
- Depth and warmth, such as burgundy, deep green, navy, or soft neutrals.

### Common Mistakes
- Choosing an outfit that limits movement or feels unfamiliar.
- Over-dressing for a casual venue.

## College / University

### Formality
- Low. Practical and comfortable for a long day.

### Clothing
- T-shirts, casual shirts, jeans, joggers, kurtis, layered hoodies, light jackets.

### Footwear
- Sneakers, sturdy sandals, comfortable flats.

### Accessories
- Backpack, watch, simple caps or scarves.

### Color Direction
- Easy neutrals plus favorite colors; layers that can move between air-conditioned classrooms and outdoor heat.

### Common Mistakes
- Footwear that does not suit long walks.
- Overly delicate fabrics for daily use.

## Travel

### Formality
- Low. Comfort, layers and practicality lead.

### Clothing
- Breathable tops, easy trousers or joggers, wrinkle-tolerant fabrics, light layers for air conditioning.

### Footwear
- Comfortable sneakers or slip-ons that are easy to remove at security.

### Accessories
- Crossbody bag, scarf or stole as an extra layer, sunglasses, simple watch.

### Color Direction
- Mid-tone or darker colors that hide small stains; a neutral base makes mixing easy.

### Common Mistakes
- New, unworn-in shoes.
- Tight waistbands for long journeys.
- Too many separate outfits instead of interchangeable pieces.

## Beach / Resort

### Formality
- Low. Light and breathable.

### Clothing
- Linen shirts, cotton kurtas, shorts, sundresses, cover-ups, resort co-ords.

### Footwear
- Flip-flops, slides, light sandals.

### Accessories
- Sunglasses, hat, light scarf, water-resistant bag.

### Color Direction
- Light and bright tones, with whites and pastels reflecting sunlight.

### Common Mistakes
- Dark, heavy fabrics in heat.
- Delicate footwear on wet sand.

## Party / Night Out

### Formality
- Medium. Expressive and event-driven.

### Clothing
- Fitted shirts, blazers, dark denim, party dresses, jumpsuits, sequined or textured pieces, fusion outfits.

### Footwear
- Boots, polished loafers, heels, statement sneakers.

### Accessories
- Watch, one statement piece of jewelry, small clutch.

### Color Direction
- Darker tones, jewel tones or metallic accents; contrast appears stronger in low light.

### Common Mistakes
- Uncomfortable footwear for dancing or standing.
- Adding multiple statement pieces at once.

## Indoor Events

### Formality
- Depends on the event; venues are often air-conditioned.

### Clothing
- A light layer such as a jacket, shawl or stole is often practical.

### Footwear
- Polished shoes suited to smooth floors and carpets.

### Accessories
- Pocket squares, clutches or subtle jewelry.

### Color Direction
- Slightly deeper tones read well under artificial lighting.

### Common Mistakes
- Ignoring strong air conditioning.
- Assuming lighting will show colors the way daylight does.

## Outdoor Events

### Formality
- Depends on the event and weather.

### Clothing
- Breathable fabrics, lighter colors in heat, layers for changing temperatures.

### Footwear
- Comfortable, stable, weather-suitable shoes. Avoid thin heels on grass or gravel.

### Accessories
- Sunglasses, hat, sunscreen-friendly clothing coverage, a compact bag.

### Color Direction
- Light and mid-tone colors for heat; earthy or deeper tones for cooler weather.

### Common Mistakes
- Not planning for sun, dust, rain or uneven ground.

## Indian Festivals and Ceremonies

Customs differ widely by region, family and community. Treat these as broad guidance and defer to local tradition and family preference.

### Diwali
- Festive and celebratory; often layered with family gatherings and evening lighting.
- Clothing: kurtas, sarees, lehengas, salwar suits, Indo-western sets.
- Color direction: rich and warm, including gold, maroon, deep green and jewel tones.

### Pongal
- A harvest festival celebrated mostly in Tamil Nadu, often with morning or daytime rituals.
- Clothing: cotton or silk sarees, veshtis (dhotis) with shirts or angavastram, cotton kurtas.
- Color direction: traditional warm tones such as yellow, orange, cream, and earthy colors with gold borders.
- Breathable fabrics are practical in warm weather.

### Onam
- A Kerala festival with a strong tradition of off-white or cream with gold-bordered kasavu clothing.
- Clothing: kasavu sarees, set-mundu, mundu with shirt or kurta.
- Color direction: cream and gold, with optional color in blouses or accessories.

### Eid
- Celebratory with family visits and community prayers.
- Clothing: kurtas, sherwanis, pathani suits, sharara or gharara sets, salwar suits, anarkalis.
- Color direction: pastels, whites, creams and jewel tones are common. Many families dress in new or festive clothing.

### Holi
- Playful and colorful, with a high chance of stains.
- Clothing: white or light cotton kurtas, old or inexpensive clothes that can absorb color.
- Footwear: sandals or shoes that can get dirty.
- Avoid delicate silks or expensive pieces.

### Traditional Weddings
- Ceremonies vary greatly by region, religion and family. Multiple functions (haldi, mehndi, sangeet, ceremony, reception) each suit different looks.
- Haldi: light cotton or yellow-toned outfits that can stain.
- Mehndi: bright, comfortable outfits, often in greens and pastels.
- Sangeet: dance-friendly outfits in lively colors.
- Ceremony: traditional attire in richer fabrics.
- Reception: see Reception section above.

### Engagements
- Semi-formal to formal, typically celebratory and photographed.
- Clothing: sarees, anarkalis, gowns, kurta sets with jackets, suits.
- Color direction: soft pastels, rich neutrals or coordinated tones; couples sometimes coordinate colors.

## Adapting to Climate, Location and Preference

- Hot and humid regions favor breathable fabrics and lighter layers.
- Cooler regions allow heavier fabrics, layering and darker palettes.
- Consider local dress norms at religious venues, where modest coverage and removable footwear may be expected.
- Personal comfort, identity and budget can outweigh any suggestion here.
- When information is missing, ask about the venue, time of day, weather, dress code and comfort needs before finalizing an outfit.
