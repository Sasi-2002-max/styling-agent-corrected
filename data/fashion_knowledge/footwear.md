# Footwear Styling Guide

This guide covers common footwear categories and how they can be paired with outfits of different formality. Pairings are guidelines. Comfort, climate, terrain and personal preference should always be considered alongside appearance.

## Choosing Footwear: General Principles

- Footwear formality should be close to the outfit's formality.
- Comfort matters more for events with long standing, walking or dancing.
- Weather affects material choice: leather and suede suit dry conditions, while rubber soles and synthetics cope better with rain.
- Shoes anchor the outfit visually, so their color and shape affect how the whole look reads.

## Sneakers

### Casual Pairing
- Work with jeans, chinos, shorts, joggers, casual dresses and kurtis.
- Athletic or chunky styles suit relaxed and sporty outfits.

### Smart Casual Pairing
- Clean leather or minimal sneakers in white, off-white, grey or black pair with tailored trousers and unstructured jackets.

### Formal and Wedding Pairing
- Generally not suited to formal settings.
- Some modern or fusion events accept minimal, embellished or all-white sneakers with Indo-western outfits. Check the event's tone.

### Notes
- Keep them clean; worn or dirty sneakers can undercut a polished outfit.

## Loafers

### Casual and Smart Casual Pairing
- Suit chinos, tailored shorts, dark jeans, midi dresses and kurta sets.
- Penny, tassel and suede styles offer different levels of dressiness.

### Formal Pairing
- Sleek leather loafers can work with less rigid business and evening looks.
- Traditional business formal usually favors laced shoes.

### Wedding and Traditional Pairing
- Embellished, velvet or patterned loafers can work with Indo-western looks and sherwanis at less traditional events.

## Oxford Shoes

### Description
- Closed-lacing shoes with a clean, streamlined shape.

### Formal Pairing
- Suit business formal, suits, formal weddings and evening events.

### Smart Casual Pairing
- Brown or tan Oxfords can work with chinos and blazers.

### Notes
- Black is most conventional for dark formal outfits, and brown adds warmth for daytime or smart casual wear.

## Derby Shoes

### Description
- Open-lacing shoes that look slightly more relaxed than Oxfords.

### Pairing
- Suit office wear, smart casual, semi-formal and many wedding contexts.
- Work well with chinos, tailored trousers and kurta with churidar or trousers.

### Notes
- Wider fit and easier lacing make them practical for people with broader feet.

## Boots

### Casual Pairing
- Chelsea, combat and chukka boots suit jeans, chinos and casual layering.

### Smart Casual Pairing
- Suede or leather Chelsea and chukka boots pair with tailored trousers and jackets.

### Weather and Comfort
- Suit cooler weather and city evenings.
- Less practical in hot, humid conditions.

### Formal and Wedding
- Generally not formal, though sleek Chelsea boots can work at less strict evening events.

## Sandals

### Casual Pairing
- Flat sandals and slides suit shorts, casual dresses, beach and travel wear.

### Smart Casual Pairing
- Leather or structured sandals can work with lightweight trousers or kurta sets in warm weather.

### Formal and Wedding Pairing
- Dressy sandals with embellishment or metallic finish suit festive and traditional outfits.

### Notes
- Good for hot climates. Consider foot comfort and support for long walking.

## Flats

### Casual and Smart Casual Pairing
- Ballet flats, loafers and pointed flats work with dresses, kurtis, trousers and skirts.

### Office Pairing
- Closed flats in neutral tones suit business casual settings.

### Wedding and Festive Pairing
- Embellished flats work with sarees, lehengas and suits when comfort matters.

### Notes
- Cushioned soles or insoles help for long events.

## Heels

### Description
- Includes block heels, stilettos, wedges and platforms.

### Casual and Smart Casual
- Low block heels and wedges pair with dresses, skirts and tailored trousers.

### Formal and Wedding
- Pumps, embellished heels and sandals suit formal and festive outfits.

### Comfort Considerations
- Heel height, width and platform affect comfort and stability.
- Block heels and wedges offer more stability on grass and uneven ground.
- Choosing a heel height the wearer is comfortable in matters more than height itself.

## Formal Shoes

### Description
- A category including Oxfords, derbies, monk straps, closed leather shoes and formal pumps.

### Pairing
- Suits, formal trousers, formal sarees and business formal outfits.

### Material and Color
- Polished leather in black or dark brown is standard for formal use.
- Match the belt to the shoe color when a belt is worn.

## Traditional Indian Footwear

### Juttis
- Flat, often embroidered, with a pointed or rounded toe.
- Pair well with kurtas, salwar suits, sarees, lehengas and Indo-western outfits.
- Embroidery and color can be matched with or contrasted to the outfit's accents.

### Mojaris
- Closely related to juttis, often with a heavier decorative finish.
- Suit weddings and festive looks with sherwanis, kurta sets and dhoti-style outfits.

### Kolhapuris
- Handcrafted leather sandals from Maharashtra and neighboring regions.
- Suit casual kurtas, festive wear, kurtis, and relaxed Indo-western outfits.
- They soften with wear and suit warm weather.

### Traditional Sandals
- Includes leather chappals, embellished sandals and regional styles.
- Suitable for festive and daily traditional wear, depending on the venue and customs.

### Religious and Home Venues
- Footwear is often removed at temples and some homes. Choose easy-to-remove options for those occasions.

## Casual Pairing Summary

- Sneakers, flat sandals, kolhapuris, casual loafers and flats.
- Match the relaxed nature of the outfit and consider walking comfort.

## Smart Casual Pairing Summary

- Clean sneakers, loafers, derbies, Chelsea boots, neat flats and low block heels.

## Formal Pairing Summary

- Oxfords, formal derbies, pumps, closed formal shoes.

## Wedding Pairing Summary

- Mojaris, juttis, embellished formal shoes, dressy sandals and heels.
- Prioritize comfort for long events.

## Traditional Outfit Pairing

- Sarees: dressy sandals, heels, flats or embellished juttis.
- Lehengas: heels, embellished flats or juttis, chosen for comfort and hem length.
- Kurtas and kurta sets: juttis, mojaris, kolhapuris, loafers or sneakers, depending on formality.
- Sherwanis: mojaris, embellished shoes or formal leather shoes.
- Indo-western outfits: loafers, boots or minimal sneakers depending on styling.

## Color Coordination

- Neutral footwear such as tan, brown, black, white and beige pairs with most outfits.
- Matching shoes to the belt and bag creates a tidy line.
- Color footwear can serve as the accent color of an outfit.
- Metallic footwear can act as a substitute for jewelry accents.
- For traditional outfits, footwear can match the embroidery or border color.

## Material Coordination

- Leather suits formal and smart casual settings.
- Suede suits dry, cooler settings and tends to look softer and less formal.
- Canvas and knit suit casual wear.
- Embroidered fabrics and velvet suit festive or wedding wear.
- Rubber soles are practical for wet weather and long walking.

## Footwear and Trouser or Dress Proportions

- Trousers that break slightly at the shoe pair well with chunkier shoes; slim ankle-length trousers pair well with sleeker shoes.
- Cropped trousers show the ankle and shoe line, so footwear becomes more visible.
- Long dresses and lehengas can cover the shoes, so comfort and stability matter more than style.
- Short dresses and skirts pair with almost any footwear; the choice affects formality more than proportion.
- Chunky shoes can balance wide-leg trousers and larger silhouettes.

## Comfort and Weather

- Hot and humid conditions favor breathable materials and open styles.
- Monsoon calls for water-resistant soles and quick-drying materials.
- Cooler weather allows closed shoes, boots and heavier soles.
- Long events benefit from cushioned insoles and broken-in shoes.

## Styling Guidelines for an AI Stylist

- Match footwear formality to the outfit first, then consider color and material.
- Ask about comfort needs for long events, walking and weather.
- Offer one formal and one relaxed option when the occasion is ambiguous.
- For traditional outfits, consider the hem length and venue customs.
- Treat pairings as suggestions and respect user preferences.
